Global Honors & Cultural Impact

In 2012, Kohli was recognized as one of the best-dressed men by GQ magazine, appearing alongside international figures including Barack Obama. In 2018, National Geographic produced a feature documentary celebrating his cricket career.

On the eve of the 2019 World Cup, Madame Tussauds unveiled his lifelike wax statue in London. Later that year, the Delhi & District Cricket Association honoured him with a named stand at the Feroz Shah Kotla Stadium (Arun Jaitley Stadium), making him the youngest cricketer in history to receive such recognition. In November 2019, Disney+ Hotstar released Super V, an animated superhero series based on his fictionalized youth.

🥗

Physical Fitness & Vegetarian Diet

In 2018, Kohli disclosed his transition to a vegetarian diet to combat a cervical spine condition triggered by elevated uric acid levels that was impacting his finger mobility and batting grip.

He has clarified that while he abstains from meat to maintain peak cardiovascular endurance and metabolic recovery, his regimen is not vegan as he continues to consume nutrient-dense dairy products. His intense functional workouts, agility drills, and strict nutritional discipline revolutionized the fitness standards of the entire Indian national cricket team.

💍

Personal Life & Heritage

Kohli's relationship with Bollywood actress Anushka Sharma began in 2013 after they met during a commercial shoot for Clear shampoo. Coined "Virushka" by media, the couple married in an intimate ceremony in Florence, Italy on 11 December 2017. They welcomed their daughter Vamika on 11 January 2021 and son Akaay on 15 February 2024.

Kohli embraces personal rituals on the field, including donning white shoes, wearing a black wristband, and wearing a sacred kara bangle on his right arm since 2012. He bears tattoos of Lord Shiva, his parents' names, and his Indian ODI and Test cap numbers.

💼

Commercial Investments & Brands

Beyond sports, Kohli has built a diversified business portfolio. He became co-owner of the Indian Super League football club FC Goa in 2014, and partnered with Universal Sportsbiz to launch WROGN, a men's casual wear label.

In 2015, he committed ₹90 crore to establish Chisel, a premier chain of fitness centers across India, and invested in the UAE Royals (tennis) and Bengaluru Yodhas (pro wrestling). In 2017, he partnered with Puma to create the athletic lifestyle brand One8, followed by luxury hospitality ventures Nueva and the One8 Commune restaurant bar chain.
